## Original Collapsi material

**Collapsi was invented by Mark S. Ball of Riffle Shuffle & Roll.** The official game page is <https://riffleshuffleandroll.itch.io/collapsi>.

The original game, its name, and any text or other material attributable to Mark S. Ball remain subject to his rights. Nothing in this repository claims ownership of Collapsi or relicenses Mark S. Ball's material. Public statements by the designer request credit as the designer and a link to the official Collapsi page; both are provided throughout this repository.

The original rulebook identifies itself as **Collapsi v1.3.1**, last updated July 9, 2025. In its “A Note to Web Developers and 3D Modelers,” Mark Ball permits web apps and 3D-printable implementations but says to “keep it demonetized” and that Collapsi “should always be free to play.” The note also requires credit to Mark Ball as designer and a link to the Collapsi itch.io page, and requests a YouTube link where possible.

That permission is expressly addressed to web developers and 3D modelers. It supports the conservative noncommercial approach used here, but it is not treated as a blanket Creative Commons license for the original rulebook, translations, or every possible derivative work.

## Why this is not a blanket license

A contributor can license only material for which they hold the necessary rights. Therefore CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 does not automatically cover pre-existing Collapsi material merely because it appears beside or is described by covered Revival material. If Mark S. Ball later grants a documented compatible license or permission, record its exact source and scope here before changing this notice.
